Chromebook Chrome OS Optimization Guide

This Progressive Web App client is specifically optimized for Chrome OS on 11-15" Chromebook displays. Install it directly to your launcher to enjoy full screen app behavior, offline state caching, and rapid launch times.

1

Install to Shelf

Click the "Install App" button in the top navigation bar or click Chrome's three-dot menu icon in the upper right and select Save and share > Install StudentSquare.

2

Pin to Launcher Shelf

On your Chromebook Shelf (bottom taskbar), right-click (two-finger tap) the StudentSquare icon and choose Pin to shelf for one-touch access.

3

Enable Notifications

Click "Test Alert" in the Messages tab to grant desktop notifications for instant updates from teachers and school administration.
